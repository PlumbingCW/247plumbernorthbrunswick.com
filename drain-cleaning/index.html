When every drain in the house runs slow or sewage comes up in the lowest tub, you need emergency drain cleaning in North Brunswick, NJ now, not after the weekend. Our 24/7 line is answered at night, and we offer same-day appointments when they are available.

While you wait, stop running water. Every flush, shower and dishwasher cycle pushes more water toward a blocked line. Pull up any rugs near floor drains and keep children and pets away from standing water in the basement.

Drain cleaning means removing the blockage and confirming the pipe flows again. For stubborn or repeat clogs we go further with hydro jetting or a sewer camera inspection, because a line that clogs twice is telling you something.

Urgent situations

When A Clogged Drain Is An Emergency

Not every clog needs a midnight call. A single slow bathroom sink can wait for a morning appointment. These situations should not wait:

Call the 24/7 line now if

  • ✓Sewage or gray water is coming up through a tub, shower or basement floor drain.
  • ✓More than one fixture is backing up at the same time, which points to the main line.
  • ✓A toilet flush makes the shower or sink gurgle or rise.
  • ✓Water is near electrical panels, a furnace, a water heater burner or a finished basement.
  • ✓The blockage appeared right after heavy rain, which can mean a problem outside the house.

If the backup is only at one fixture, turn off the supply to that fixture and tell us which one. That detail changes the tool we put on the truck.

Method

Snake, Cable Or Jet: What Actually Clears It

A hand auger or drum machine cuts through a soft clog such as hair and soap. It punches a hole in the blockage, which is fine for a tub drain and often not enough for a main line, where the pipe stays coated with grease or roots after the hole opens.

A cable with a cutting head scrapes the pipe wall and clears roots from clay or cast iron lines. Cabling is the usual first step on a main line. If the line still narrows within weeks, step up to high-pressure water, which scours the wall. That job has its own page, hydro jetting.

We tell you which tool the job needs and why. If a cheaper method will hold, we say so. If it will not, you hear that before we start.

Kitchen

Kitchen Sink Clogs In North Brunswick Homes

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines. Fat, coffee grounds and starchy food cool in the pipe and build a hard coating that narrows the line a little more each month. A garbage disposal grinds food but does not stop grease from sticking further downstream.

If the sink drains in a slow circle and the dishwasher leaves water in the bottom, the blockage is usually past the sink trap. We cable the branch line, then check the dishwasher drain hose and air gap. If your disposal hums and the sink stays full, see garbage disposal repair, since the jam may be in the unit itself.

To slow the next clog, wipe pans before washing, run hot water for 30 seconds after the disposal, and keep fat out of the drain. Chemical openers can soften a clog but they can also sit in a standing pipe and damage older fittings.

Bathroom

Tub And Shower Drains

Hair and soap make a plug just below the tub or shower drain. Pulling the stopper and clearing the strainer fixes many of these. If the water still stands, the blockage is farther down, and we cable it from the cleanout or through the drain opening.

A drain that clears but then leaves a damp ceiling or musty smell below it may be leaking. A cracked drain line behaves differently from a clog, and clearing it harder will not help. In that case our leak detection service finds the crack so the repair stays small.

Local conditions

After Heavy Rain: Floor Drains And Main Lines

North Brunswick sits in the Lawrence Brook watershed, and the township saw what a big storm does on September 1, 2021, when floodwater from Tropical Storm Ida damaged the municipal complex at 710 Hermann Road. A basement floor drain that rises during heavy rain is a different problem from a drain that backs up on a dry day.

If your drain rises only in storms, the line may be taking in groundwater or overloaded outside the house, and a cable will not cure that. Tell us when it happens. We check the line with a camera, look for cracks and offset joints, and talk through options, including a backwater valve if the line allows one.

Changing a drain layout is covered by the New Jersey plumbing subcode, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, so ask the North Brunswick building department before moving a trap or a vent.

Pricing

What Decides The Price Of Clearing A Drain

Where the blockage sits

A fixture trap is quick. A main line behind the foundation takes more time and a larger machine.

What is in the pipe

Soap and hair clear easily. Roots and hardened grease need cutting heads or a jet.

Access to the line

A cleanout makes the job easier. Without one, we may need to pull a toilet or work from a vent.

Camera inspection

A camera adds time but shows the cause, so a repeat clog does not cost you twice.

Hour of the visit

After-hours emergency calls cost more than a scheduled visit. You hear the price before work starts.

Insurance, honestly

Homeowners policies generally cover sudden and accidental water damage, not the cost of clearing a clog. Some policies add sewer backup coverage as an extra. Ask your insurer before you need it.
