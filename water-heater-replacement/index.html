Emergency Water Heater Replacement in North Brunswick, NJ

When the tank is leaking, rusted through or past repair, replacement is the safe fix. We size the new heater, remove the old one and test the install before we leave.

Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 13, 2026

A leaking tank is a leading reason for emergency water heater replacement in North Brunswick, NJ. Our 24/7 line is open at night, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Shut off the cold water supply valve on top of the tank and, for an electric unit, switch off its breaker. For gas, turn the control to off and close the gas supply valve at the heater. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 first. Put towels and a bucket by the tank and keep power cords out of standing water.

A new heater is a big household purchase, so we explain the choices in plain words. If the failure was minor, repair might do. If you want long-term savings, look at a tankless unit. For a planned upgrade with no emergency, see water heater installation.

Urgent

When Replacement Is The Only Answer

Replace rather than repair when

  • ✓Water leaks from the tank body, seams or the bottom.
  • ✓The heater is well past a typical service life and has rusty water or repeated failures.
  • ✓A repair would cost a large share of a new unit.
  • ✓The unit is undersized for a household that has grown.
  • ✓A gas unit has a corroded flue, damaged venting or a failed burner assembly.

Water damage grows by the hour. A heater that has already leaked a small amount is a hazard, because the water can reach electrical parts or soak wood framing.

Choices

For an emergency, a like-for-like tank is often the fastest path back to hot water. A tankless swap usually takes planning for venting and gas capacity. We do not push either one. We explain what your house can support.

Sizing

Sizing And Fuel

Size is about how much hot water the household uses in a busy hour. A family of five with two bathrooms and a dishwasher needs more capacity than a couple in a townhouse. An undersized tank runs cold in the middle of a shower.

Fuel follows what the house has. Natural gas is common in North Brunswick, and PSE&G is the gas utility. Switching from gas to electric, or the reverse, adds work, so we ask early.

During the job

Living Without Hot Water For A Day

A replacement is usually a same-day job, though a fuel change or tankless swap takes longer. Plan for cold water in the meantime. Heat water on the stove for dishes and washing up.

Keep children and pets away from the work area. Clear a path to the heater so the plumber does not have to move boxes while the tank is draining. A clear path saves time and keeps the job on schedule.

Efficiency

Heat Pump And High-Efficiency Options

A heat pump water heater pulls heat from the surrounding air and uses far less electricity than a standard electric tank. It needs open space, some airflow and a drain for condensate, so it suits a roomy basement better than a closet.

High-efficiency gas tanks and condensing units use less fuel but need venting suited to cooler exhaust. Some utilities and state programs offer rebates on efficient models, so check the current offers before you buy. We will tell you plainly whether the savings justify the extra purchase price for your household.

Process

What Happens During A Replacement

  1. 1
    Shut down and drain

    We turn off power or gas and water, then drain the old tank safely.

  2. 2
    Remove and haul away

    The old heater comes out and leaves with us.

  3. 3
    Set and connect

    The new heater is set on a clean pan or stand, connected with new fittings and venting, and fitted with an expansion tank if the system needs one.

  4. 4
    Fire up and test

    We check for leaks, check the temperature setting, check venting on a gas unit and walk you through the controls.

Permit rules vary, so ask the North Brunswick building department what your swap needs. New Jersey's plumbing subcode follows the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code, and a licensed plumber knows what that requires.

Safety

Safety Details That Matter

A temperature and pressure relief valve with a proper discharge pipe is not optional. A gas heater needs correct venting and combustion air, and a carbon monoxide alarm nearby is a good idea. We check each of these on every install.

The township's water measured about 3.9 grains per gallon in its 2022 report. That is moderately hard, so scale builds slowly, but a yearly flush still helps a new tank.

Pricing

What Affects The Cost Of Replacement

Heater type and size

A 40-gallon tank costs less than a large tankless or hybrid unit.

Venting and gas work

A new vent or a larger gas line adds labor and parts.

Code upgrades

An expansion tank, a drain pan or a corrected relief line may be needed.

Access

A basement stairway or a tight closet takes more time.

Hour of the visit

A night or holiday call costs more than a scheduled visit.

Insurance, honestly

The heater itself is a wear item and is rarely covered. If a sudden burst damaged your floors, your policy may cover that damage. Photograph the tank and the area before cleanup.
