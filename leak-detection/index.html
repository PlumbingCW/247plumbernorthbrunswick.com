If water is showing up where it should not be, call for emergency leak detection in North Brunswick, NJ. Our 24/7 line is open at night, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Shut off the main water valve if you hear running water, see a stain growing on a ceiling or find a wet floor. The main shut-off sits near the meter or where the line enters the house. Turn off power to any soaked area only if you can reach the breaker safely.

Finding the leak is half the job. Once located, we fix it ourselves or hand off to burst pipe repair, water line repair or sewer line repair as needed.

Symptoms

Signs Of A Hidden Leak

Call us if you notice

  • ✓A water bill that jumped without any change in how you live.
  • ✓A meter that keeps spinning when every fixture is off.
  • ✓A stain, bubble or peeling paint on a ceiling or wall.
  • ✓A warm patch on a floor, which can point to a hot water line under the slab.
  • ✓A musty smell, mold spots or warped baseboards.
  • ✓The sound of running water when everything is off.

A leak does not always show itself. Water can travel along a joist or inside a wall before it reaches a place you can see.

Do it yourself

The Meter Test You Can Do Now

  1. 1
    Shut everything off

    Turn off all taps and appliances, including the ice maker and irrigation.

  2. 2
    Read the meter

    Note the numbers or watch the small leak indicator dial.

  3. 3
    Wait an hour

    Do not use any water.

  4. 4
    Read it again

    If the number moved or the dial turned, water is going somewhere.

Tell us what you saw. If the meter moves with the house valve closed, the leak is probably in the line from the street. If it stops, the leak is inside the house.

Next steps

After We Find It: Repair, Drying And Insurance

Finding the leak is step one. Step two is repair, and step three is drying out anything that got wet. Drywall and insulation that stay damp for more than a couple of days can grow mold, so call a restoration company if the area is large.

Photograph the damage and keep the failed pipe section if you can. Call your insurer early, because many policies have notice requirements. We give you a written description of what we found and where, which helps with a claim.

Prevention

Preventing The Next Leak

Water pressure above a safe range stresses every joint in the house. A cheap gauge on an outdoor spigot tells you your pressure. If it is high, a pressure regulator is a modest fix that protects fixtures and the water heater.

Replace old rubber washing machine hoses with braided ones, and check them yearly. Know where your main shut-off is, and test the valve so it turns when you need it. Those three habits stop a surprising number of emergencies.

Where leaks hide

Slab Leaks, Wall Leaks And Buried Lines

A slab leak is a break in a pipe under a concrete floor. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, a constant hiss and a rising bill. Acoustic equipment listens at the floor and traces the pipe. Repair options range from a spot dig to rerouting the line overhead.

A leak inside a wall or ceiling is often a small pinhole in copper or a failed joint. We use thermal imaging to keep the opening small. If the leak is on the buried line from the street, see water line repair for how those jobs run.

Irrigation lines and crawlspace pipes are checked too. Tell us where you suspect the problem.

High bills

Why High Bills Matter In North Brunswick

North Brunswick's water and sewer office at the municipal building on Hermann Road handles billing questions, and a sudden jump is worth raising with them as well as with a plumber. A leaking toilet flapper can waste a lot of water without making any noise.

Check toilets with a few drops of food coloring in the tank. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking. See toilet repair for the fix. According to the township's 2022 water quality report, the highest annual chlorine average was 2.40 ppm against a limit of 4.0, which describes water at the treatment plant and says nothing about the pipes inside your walls.

Pricing

What Affects The Cost Of Leak Detection

Location of the leak

A wall pinhole costs less to open than a leak under a slab.

Equipment needed

Acoustic, thermal and camera work take different time.

Size of the property

A larger house has more pipe to trace.

Access

A finished basement ceiling or a tight crawlspace slows the work.

Repair after detection

Finding the leak and fixing it are separate line items, and you decide on the second.

Insurance, honestly

Many homeowners policies cover the water damage from a sudden leak but not the cost of finding or repairing the pipe itself. Some policies exclude slow leaks. Ask your insurer, and keep photos and bills.
