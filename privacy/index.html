Information

What We Collect

This website has no forms. If you call us, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of your plumbing problem. If you email us, we collect your email address and what you write.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, browser type and the pages you visit. We use this information to keep the site working and secure.

Use

How We Use It

We use call and email information to schedule and perform plumbing work, send invoices, answer questions and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with people who help us do the work, such as suppliers or insurers, when needed to complete a job or when the law requires it.

Cookies

Cookies And Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics tools later, we will update this page to describe them.

Rights

Your Choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, within the limits of law and our need to keep job records. Write to contact@247plumbernorthbrunswick.com or call (732) 466-1098.

Changes
