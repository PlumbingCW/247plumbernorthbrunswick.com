Emergency Water Line Repair in North Brunswick, NJ

The water service line runs underground from the meter to your house. When it fails you get a soggy yard, a sudden drop in pressure or no water at all.

A broken service line calls for emergency water line repair in North Brunswick, NJ. Our 24/7 line is open at night, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Close the main valve inside the house. If the leak is outside, a wet patch may still grow until the curb stop is closed, so tell us where the water is showing. Do not dig near the line yourself, because buried gas and electric lines may be close.

The service line is the pipe that brings water from the utility main to your house. Interior leaks are a separate matter, see leak detection and burst pipe repair.

Symptoms

Signs The Service Line Is Failing

Symptoms of a service line problem

  • ✓A wet, soft or sinking patch of lawn between the street and the house, even in dry weather.
  • ✓A sudden drop in water pressure at every fixture.
  • ✓A meter that keeps spinning when all indoor fixtures are off.
  • ✓Water that is dirty, cloudy or sandy after a repair on the street.
  • ✓A water bill that rose without a change in use.
  • ✓The sound of running water near the foundation.

The meter test on our leak detection page is a fast way to separate an outside leak from an inside leak.

Ownership

Who Owns Which Part Of The Line

In general, the utility owns the main and often the connection to the meter or curb stop, and the owner is responsible for the line from there to the house. The exact point varies, so ask the township water office before you call anyone to dig.

North Brunswick's water utility is run by Veolia for the township, and the office is on the second floor of the municipal building at 710 Hermann Road. For water emergencies, the township lists its own line, and we recommend confirming with them where your responsibility ends. We repair the private side.

Process

What Happens On A Repair Call

  1. 1
    Shut off and locate

    We close the water and locate the line with listening gear.

  2. 2
    Mark-outs

    Excavation calls for utility mark-outs through New Jersey 811 before digging.

  3. 3
    Repair or replace

    We repair the broken section or replace the run, then pressure-test it.

  4. 4
    Restore and flush

    Water is turned back on, lines are flushed, and the trench is backfilled or protected.

Ask the North Brunswick building department whether your repair needs a permit or inspection.

Water quality

Water Quality After A Repair

After any repair, flush the lines by running cold taps until the water runs clear. Remove faucet aerators and clean them out, since small debris can collect there.

The township's 2022 water quality report found zero of 31 samples above the lead action level and zero of 31 above the copper action level. That reflects the utility's testing. The condition of the private line is a different question, which is why we check the material on every service line job.

Digging for a service line calls for New Jersey 811 mark-outs, and standard practice is to confirm with the township water office where your responsibility begins.

Pricing

What Affects Water Line Repair Pricing

Length and depth

A short shallow repair costs far less than a long deep run.

Surface above the line

Driveways, walkways and trees add restoration.

Pipe material

Copper, PEX and HDPE service pipe differ in cost.

Method

Spot repair, trenchless and open trench differ widely.

Permits and mark-outs

Handled before excavation.

Insurance, honestly

Many homeowners policies exclude the underground service line itself. Some insurers and local programs offer service line coverage as an add-on. Ask yours before you need it.
