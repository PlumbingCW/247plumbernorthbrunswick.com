A drip wastes water and a failed supply line can flood a cabinet. We repair and replace faucets, valves, supply lines and sink drains.

Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 13, 2026

A faucet that sprays or a supply line that lets go under the sink calls for emergency faucet repair in North Brunswick, NJ. The 24/7 line is open, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Close the stop valve under the sink by turning it clockwise. If there is no stop valve or it will not turn, shut off the main water. Then clear the cabinet so water is not soaking stored items.

Most faucet jobs are quick. When the fixture is old or corroded, replacement is usually smarter than another repair, and our plumbing installation page covers new fixtures. A sink that will not drain is a drain cleaning job.

Urgent

Urgent Faucet And Sink Problems

Call the 24/7 line if

  • ✓A supply line is spraying or dripping steadily under the sink.
  • ✓The faucet body is cracked or the handle has come loose with water running.
  • ✓The stop valve is stuck and water cannot be turned off at the fixture.
  • ✓A kitchen or bathroom sink is leaking into the cabinet or floor below.
  • ✓Water is coming through the ceiling under an upstairs sink.

Cabinet leaks

Leaks Under The Sink

Most under-sink leaks come from the supply connectors, the stop valve, the drain slip nuts or the trap. Dry the area, wrap a dry paper towel around each joint and watch which one gets wet first.

Corroded braided supply lines can burst. If yours are old, replace them before they fail. We also check the stop valves, which can seize in older homes and fail to shut off in an emergency.

Flow

Low Flow And Mineral Buildup

A faucet that sputters or has weak flow may have a clogged aerator or cartridge. North Brunswick's water measured 67.5 ppm hardness in the 2022 report, about 3.9 grains per gallon, so mineral deposits do build over time.

Soaking the aerator in vinegar clears scale. If flow is poor at every fixture, the problem may be pressure or pipes, and our repiping page explains that path.

Decision

Repair Or Replace

A newer faucet with a worn cartridge is a good repair. A faucet that is pitted, loose or leaking from the body is cheaper to replace. We carry common faucets and can install one you provide.

A leaking kitchen faucet is also a chance to check the garbage disposal connection. See garbage disposal repair if the disposal is leaking too. Supply connections and fixtures fall under the New Jersey plumbing subcode, the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, so ask before a swap changes the pipework behind the wall.

Pricing

What Affects The Cost Of Faucet Repair

Faucet type and brand

Common cartridges are inexpensive. Specialty fixtures cost more.

Corroded stops or lines

Replacing a seized stop valve adds work.

Access

A tight vanity or a wall-mounted fixture takes longer.

Fixture supplied

A new faucet costs more than a repair kit.

Hour of the visit

A night or holiday call costs more than a daytime appointment.

Insurance, honestly

A worn faucet is a maintenance item and is not covered. If a supply line burst and soaked your floors, the water damage may be covered. Ask your insurer.
