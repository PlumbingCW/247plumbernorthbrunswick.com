Sewage in a basement drain or tub is an emergency sewer line cleaning situation in North Brunswick, NJ, and our 24/7 line is open for it. We offer same-day appointments when they are available.

While you wait, stop using water, leave the washing machine and dishwasher off, and keep children and pets out of the affected room. Do not wade into sewage water and do not run a wet vacuum on it.

Sewer line cleaning clears the line that carries all the house's waste toward the street. It overlaps with drain cleaning, but it focuses on the main line, the cleanout and the underground run, where a camera is the main tool. If the camera shows damage, we move to sewer line repair.

Sewer backup

Sewer Backup: What To Do First

A sewer backup is not the same as a slow sink. When the main line is blocked, waste has nowhere to go, so water rises where it can, usually a basement floor drain, a lowest-level tub or a laundry standpipe.

  1. 1
    Stop adding water

    No flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher until the line is clear.

  2. 2
    Protect the area

    Move boxes and electronics off the floor and keep the area ventilated.

  3. 3
    Find the cleanout

    Tell us whether you have a cleanout near the foundation, in the basement or in the yard. It saves time.

  4. 4
    Call the 24/7 line

    Report which fixtures are backing up and whether it happened right after heavy rain.

Causes

Why Main Lines Block

Roots, grease, a collapsed pipe section and items that should not be flushed are the four common causes. Flushable wipes are a big one. They do not break down like paper and gather on any rough spot.

Older homes in the area may have clay, cast iron or fiber pipe that has cracked or shifted over decades. That is one reason a camera matters. A cable can open the line but cannot tell you the pipe is breaking.

Storm backups

Basement Floor Drains After Big Storms

Heavy rain can make a drain rise even when the line is not blocked. The township's own experience on September 1, 2021, when floodwater from Ida damaged the municipal complex on Hermann Road and forced the police department to evacuate, is a reminder that this area floods.

If your drain backs up only during storms, tell us. That calls for a camera check and a conversation about backwater valves and sump pump discharge, which we cover on our sump pump repair page.

Clarifying

Cleaning Versus Septic Pumping

Most homes in North Brunswick are on a public sewer, so a backup is a plumbing problem rather than a full tank. If you are unsure whether your property has a septic tank, say so when you call. Septic tank pumping is a separate service from line cleaning.

Plumbing codes generally call for accessible cleanouts, and New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, so ask the building department what applies to your house before adding one.

Pricing

What Goes Into A Sewer Cleaning Quote

Distance to the blockage

A blockage close to the cleanout takes less time than one far down the run.

Tool needed

A cable is the lowest cost. A jet or a large machine adds time and equipment.

Camera inspection

Recommended after every main line backup so you can see the pipe.

Access

A cleanout helps. Pulling a toilet to get in adds labor.

After-hours visit

Night and holiday calls cost more than daytime appointments.

Insurance, honestly

Clearing a blocked line is normally a maintenance cost. Damage from the backup is sometimes covered if your policy has sewer backup coverage. Photograph everything before cleanup.
