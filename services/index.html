Repair

Emergency And Repair Services

These services are the ones people call for at night and on weekends. Each page explains the warning signs, what to do while you wait and what drives the price.

Drain Cleaning

Kitchen, tub and main line clogs cleared with cable, jet and camera.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, rumbling tanks, pilot faults and leaks, on gas, electric and hybrid units.

Water Heater Replacement

A leaking or worn-out tank swapped for a properly sized new heater.

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks, high bills, slab and wall leaks found without tearing out walls.

Sewer Line Repair

Cracked, rooted or collapsed laterals repaired or replaced.

Burst Pipe Repair

Flooding stopped, the break repaired and the pipe tested.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water that cleans grease, scale and roots from sound pipe.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs with venting, relief valves and code items handled.

Tankless Water Heaters

Install, descale and repair of on-demand gas and electric units.
