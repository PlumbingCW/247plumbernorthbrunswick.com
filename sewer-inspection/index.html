Sewer Camera Inspection in North Brunswick, NJ

A small waterproof camera on a flexible cable shows what is inside your sewer line: roots, cracks, bellies, offset joints and blockages. You see the footage and hear what it means.

Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 13, 2026

A sewer camera inspection in North Brunswick, NJ turns guesswork into evidence. It tells you whether a clog was a one-time event or a symptom of pipe damage, and it is a smart step before buying an older home.

Our plumbers feed the camera through a cleanout or a pulled toilet, record the run and mark where problems sit. If the pipe is dirty, sewer line cleaning or hydro jetting comes next. If the pipe is damaged, we talk through sewer line repair.

Inspections are scheduled visits. If a backup is happening right now, call the 24/7 line and we will clear it first, then camera the line.

Findings

What The Camera Finds

A camera sees what a cable cannot. Typical findings include roots entering at joints, cracks along the crown of the pipe, bellies that hold water, offset joints, fat and scale buildup, and sections that have collapsed.

We pause on each problem, note its distance from the cleanout and explain what it means. A belly that holds an inch of water is a different story from a pipe that is half-collapsed.

Timing

When To Schedule One

Good reasons for a camera inspection

  • ✓You are buying an older house and want to know what the lateral looks like before closing.
  • ✓The main line has backed up more than once.
  • ✓A cable keeps catching at the same depth.
  • ✓You see a sinkhole, soft patch or unusual greening of the yard.
  • ✓You are planning a basement bathroom or other work that adds load to the line.

Process

What The Inspection Includes

  1. 1
    Access

    We locate a cleanout or open a toilet to get the camera into the pipe.

  2. 2
    Run the line

    The camera moves toward the street while we record footage.

  3. 3

  4. 4

Ask for a copy of the footage. If a repair is recommended, you can use it to compare quotes or show it to your insurer.

Real estate

Buying Or Selling An Older Home

Many older houses around Route 1, Route 130 and Livingston Avenue were built long before plastic pipe was common. A lateral from that era might be clay, cast iron or fiber pipe, and any of them can hide trouble.

A camera inspection before closing costs little compared with a surprise repair. Share the footage with your agent or attorney, and remember that a seller can often credit repair costs when the footage shows damage. An inspection follows the same plumbing subcode, the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, that governs any repair that comes after it.

Pricing

What Affects The Price Of An Inspection

Length of run

A short run to a nearby street costs less time than a long lateral.

Access

A cleanout makes it faster. Pulling a toilet takes more time.

Pipe cleaning first

Heavy buildup may need cleaning before the camera can see the wall.

Locating

Marking depth and position adds a locating step.

Footage delivery

A recording you can keep is part of the visit.

Insurance, honestly

An inspection by itself is rarely covered. If it documents damage that your policy covers, the footage can support a claim. Ask your insurer first.
