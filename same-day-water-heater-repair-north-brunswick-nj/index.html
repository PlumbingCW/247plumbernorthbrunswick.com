Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 13, 2026

A cold shower is the quickest way to make a plumbing problem urgent. Same day water heater repair is realistic for many faults, because the parts that fail most often are small and plumbers carry them.

This guide helps you figure out what happened, what to do while you wait and how to decide between repair and replacement.

If a tank is leaking or you smell gas, skip the checklist. Close the water supply, leave the house for gas and call the 24/7 line.

Before you call

Five Quick Checks Before You Call

  1. 1
    Check the breaker or switch

    On an electric heater, look for a tripped breaker. Reset it once. If it trips again, stop.

  2. 2
    Check the pilot or ignition

    On a gas heater, see whether the pilot is lit. Follow the label to relight if it is safe.

  3. 3
    Look at the temperature setting

    A setting knocked to the lowest mark leaves water lukewarm.

  4. 4
    Check for water on the floor

    A wet base can mean a leak. Close the cold supply on top of the tank.

  5. 5
    Listen

    Rumbling, popping or hissing helps us diagnose by phone.

Decision

How To Decide Between Repair And Replacement

Look at the data plate. The serial number usually reveals the month and year of manufacture. A newer tank with a bad part is a repair. A tank that is well past a typical life, rusting at the base or leaking is a replacement.

Ask for both prices. A repair that costs a large share of a new unit is rarely wise on an old tank. Our water heater repair page lists what a plumber checks.

North Brunswick facts

North Brunswick Water And Your Tank

The Township of North Brunswick Water Utility is operated by Veolia North America under a 20-year agreement signed in 2022. Its 2022 report listed hardness of 67.5 ppm as calcium carbonate, about 3.9 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard.

Scale builds slowly at that level, but a tank that has never been flushed collects sediment on the bottom. That sediment is the rumble you hear and the reason an electric lower element burns out.

Gas safety

Gas Safety Rules You Should Know

A gas heater needs correct venting. If the flame looks yellow, soot marks appear near the draft hood or you smell gas, stop using it. Leave the house and call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 from outside.

Never relight a pilot if you smell gas. Never block the combustion air. A carbon monoxide alarm near gas appliances is inexpensive protection.

Data plate

Reading The Data Plate Before You Call

The sticker on the side of the tank lists the capacity, the fuel, the model and the serial number. Photograph it and have it ready. It lets the dispatcher guess which parts to bring.

The serial number usually encodes the month and year the tank was made. A tank near the end of its typical life may be a better candidate for replacement than for a repair, even if the problem looks small.

Meanwhile

What You Can Do While Waiting For Hot Water

If the cause is a tripped breaker or an out pilot, a single reset may bring it back, but do not keep resetting a breaker that trips again. Use the stove for dishes, and take a quick lukewarm shower from the lower elements if the tank still has some warmth.

If the tank is leaking, close the cold water valve and drain the pan or floor. If you leave a small leak alone for hours, the water can soak flooring and framing. The relief valve and its discharge pipe are code items under the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, which New Jersey adopts.
