Repiping In North Brunswick, NJ

Repiping replaces worn supply pipes with new ones. It is a planned project, and it can end a cycle of pinhole leaks, rusty water and weak pressure.

Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 13, 2026

Repiping in North Brunswick, NJ makes sense when patching one leak after another stops being practical. Houses built across the township over the decades carry copper, galvanized steel and sometimes mixed systems, and each ages differently.

This is a scheduled job, so we start with an inspection and a plain conversation about options. If a pipe is leaking today, call the 24/7 line and ask for burst pipe repair first. Repiping can follow when things are dry.

A good repipe also covers the water heater connections, shut-offs and pressure. For the heater side of the project, see water heater installation.

Symptoms

Signs Your Pipes Are Wearing Out

Symptoms that point toward repiping

  • ✓Repeated pinhole leaks in copper, often with green or white crust.
  • ✓Brown or rusty water when you first open a tap, a classic sign of galvanized steel corroding from the inside.
  • ✓Low pressure at several fixtures even though the street pressure is fine.
  • ✓Visible flaking, mineral buildup or bulging at joints.
  • ✓Water that tastes metallic.
  • ✓A history of leaks in different parts of the house.

One leak does not mean the house needs a new system. A pattern of leaks in different places does.

Process

What Repiping Involves

  1. 1
    Inspection and plan

    We map the existing lines, check pressure and discuss routes through walls, ceilings and the basement.

  2. 2
    Clear price and schedule

    You get the scope, price and timeline in writing before work starts.

  3. 3
    Rough-in

    New lines are run to each fixture while the old ones stay in service where possible, to keep water on.

  4. 4
    Cutover and test

    We switch the house to the new lines, pressure-test them and remove the old ones that are accessible.

  5. 5
    Patch and finish

    Access holes are kept small and patched so a painter or drywall pro can finish.

Most houses can stay occupied during the work, though water is off for part of a day.

Local housing

Repiping In Homes From The Postwar Boom

North Brunswick grew quickly in the postwar decades, and the township counted 15,789 housing units in the 2020 Census, up from 13,932 in 2000. A lot of that stock is old enough that the original supply lines are at or past their expected life.

If your house is among them, a camera on the drain side and a pressure test on the supply side tell us where you stand. Sometimes only part of the house needs work, and a partial repipe is the honest answer.

Decision

Repipe Or Repair? A Fair Comparison

If leaks are rare and localized, spot repairs cost less. If the pipe is corroded throughout, repairs just chase the next failure. We show you the cost of both paths, including the risk of water damage in between.

Water quality matters too. The township's 2022 report lists hardness of 67.5 ppm and zero samples above the 15 ppb lead action level.

Private plumbing inside a house is a separate question from the utility's results, so old galvanized or lead-soldered lines inside the house are worth replacing. According to N.J.S.A. 45:14C-1, plumbing is done only by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor, and the state subcode adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, so a repipe is a licensed and permitted job.

Prepare

What To Do Before A Repipe Inspection

Before we visit

  • ✓Locate and test the main shut-off valve.
  • ✓Note which fixtures have low pressure or rusty water.
  • ✓Clear access to the basement, crawlspace and under-sink cabinets.
  • ✓Photograph any stains on ceilings and walls.
  • ✓Gather past plumbing invoices if you have them.
Pricing

What Affects A Repiping Price

House size and fixture count

More bathrooms and kitchens mean more pipe.

Material choice

Copper costs more than PEX in parts and labor.

Access

Open basement ceilings are easier than finished plaster walls.

Patching and restoration

Drywall work can be included or handled by another contractor.

Permits and inspections

Ask the North Brunswick building department what your project requires.

Insurance, honestly

Insurance usually does not pay to replace worn pipe, though it may cover resulting water damage from a sudden failure. A repipe can sometimes help with insurability on an older home, so ask your agent.
