Who we are

What We Do

North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 651 Route 1 in North Brunswick, New Jersey. We handle drains, water heaters, leaks, sewer lines, boilers, sump pumps, fixtures and commercial buildings, and we answer the emergency line day and night.

We are licensed and insured. New Jersey law says plumbing work is done by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor, and licensees must show their license number on invoices and advertising. Anyone can verify a license through the Division of Consumer Affairs license verification system, and we encourage it.

The services we offer are all on the services page, and each one explains symptoms, options and what shapes the price.

Method

How We Work

Our approach is simple. You call, we ask direct questions and we tell you what to do while a plumber is on the way. On site, the plumber finds the cause, explains the options and gives you a clear price before any tool touches your plumbing.

  1. 1
    Answer

    The 24/7 line is open every day. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

  2. 2
    Diagnose

    We use cameras, pressure tests and listening gear to find the cause, not just the symptom.

  3. 3
    Quote

    You hear the price and the options before work starts.

  4. 4
    Repair and test

    The repair is tested before the plumber leaves.

We do not promise arrival times, because traffic and the job before yours are real. We do promise honest communication.

Scope

What We Handle, By Type Of Job

Drain and sewer work covers clogs, main line backups, hydro jetting and camera inspections, plus repairs when the camera shows a cracked or rooted pipe. Our drain cleaning and sewer line repair pages explain each step.

Hot water work covers repair, replacement and installation of tank, tankless and hybrid heaters. Leaks and pipes cover burst pipes, hidden leaks, water service lines and repiping. Fixtures, disposals, toilets and sump pumps round out the list, with boilers handled on the water side.

Commercial calls include restaurants, shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, with after-hours service and written invoices that landlords and insurers can use.

Pricing

Pricing And Scheduling In Plain Words

We do not post dollar figures, because the honest price depends on the pipe, the access and the hour. What we do post, on every service page, is the list of factors that move the price, so you know what to expect before you call.

When you call, the plumber explains the cause and the options, then gives you a price before work starts. Nothing begins until you agree. Emergency visits at night and on holidays cost more than daytime appointments, and we say so up front.

Planned work such as an install, a repipe or a camera inspection is scheduled at a time that suits you. Same-day appointments are offered when available, for planned jobs and repairs alike.

Neighbors

Local Roots

North Brunswick is a township of 43,905 residents in 12.31 square miles, according to the 2020 Census. It sits between New Brunswick and South Brunswick, along Route 1 and Route 130. We work in neighborhoods such as Livingston Park, Franklin Park, Black Horse and Red Lion.

The township's water comes from its own utility, operated by Veolia, with source water from the Delaware and Raritan Canal. The 2022 report listed moderately hard water at about 3.9 grains per gallon. We use that kind of detail when we talk about water heaters and fixtures.

On September 1, 2021, Tropical Storm Ida's floodwater damaged the township's municipal complex on Hermann Road. It is a reminder of what water does to a building, and our sump pump and waterproofing pages cover the plumbing side.

First call

What To Expect On Your First Call

Have the address ready, along with what is happening, where the water is going and whether you have shut anything off. Tell us if you rent and who owns the building. Landlords and property managers can approve work by phone.

If you smell gas, leave the house first. Call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 from outside. We will be here when the utility clears the building.

For everything else, call (732) 466-1098 and a person will pick up.
