Prepare

What To Have Ready

Tell the dispatcher

  • ✓The street address and the town.
  • ✓What is happening and where the water is going.
  • ✓Whether you have closed the main valve.
  • ✓Whether you own or rent, and who owns the building.
  • ✓Any smells, especially sewer or gas.

Gas smell? Leave first and call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734.

Which call

Emergency Or Scheduled?

Burst pipes, sewage backups and failed water heaters are emergencies. A fixture swap or a camera inspection can be scheduled. Either way, call the same number. If you are unsure, describe the problem and we will tell you.
