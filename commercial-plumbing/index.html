When a restaurant line backs up or a restroom floods overnight, call for emergency commercial plumbing in North Brunswick, NJ. The 24/7 line is open, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Close the main or the affected branch valve if you can, post a sign on the restroom door and keep customers out of wet areas. Photograph the damage for your insurer or landlord, and tell us the building size and who owns the space.

Commercial work often builds on the same tools as home jobs, such as hydro jetting, drain cleaning and leak detection, with added attention to code, tenants and downtime.

Kitchens

Grease, Traps And Floor Drains

Commercial kitchens along the Route 1 and Route 130 corridors send a steady stream of grease to the line. A grease trap or interceptor catches some of it, but it has to be emptied on schedule. When it is not, grease reaches the sewer.

A monthly or quarterly cleaning plan costs less than an emergency on a Saturday night. We can set up a schedule, camera the line to see how fast it builds up and advise on trap service. Grease trap pumping itself is a separate hauler job, and we coordinate with one when needed.

Code

Backflow And Code Items

Many commercial buildings need backflow prevention on the water supply, and some need annual testing. Rules vary by use and by the township. Ask the North Brunswick building department what applies to your property.

New Jersey's plumbing subcode adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition. We install and repair to that standard, and licensed work matters most for landlords and tenants who need documentation.

Prepare

Plan Ahead To Avoid Downtime

Know where your main shut-off and each branch valve are. Label them. Keep a list of the water heater age, the sewer cleanout location and the last time the line was cleaned. That one page saves minutes during an emergency.

North Brunswick's 2020 Census counted 43,905 residents and a mix of retail strips, offices and multi-unit housing. Whatever the building, the same prep applies.
