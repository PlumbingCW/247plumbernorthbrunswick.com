Need an emergency plumber in South Brunswick, NJ? Call the 24/7 line and give us the address. Our crews work out of 651 Route 1 in North Brunswick, and the township borders ours to the south.

Because South Brunswick runs its own water and sewer utility, a call about no water or low pressure may start with the utility. We help you work out which side of the meter the problem is on, then handle the part that belongs to the homeowner.

The sections below cover the housing, the water and the neighborhoods that shape plumbing calls here.

ZIP codes and villages

A Township With Three ZIP Codes

South Brunswick covers 41.02 square miles and counted 47,043 residents in 2020. It uses ZIP 08852 for Monmouth Junction, 08810 for Dayton and 08824 for Kendall Park. Other communities include Heathcote, Deans and Kingston.

A township this size has a mix of housing ages. Kendall Park began in 1957 as a planned community of about 1,500 homes, so its houses are now old enough that the original pipes deserve attention. Dayton and Monmouth Junction include both older homes and later developments. Tell us which village you are in, because the age of a house often tells us what we will find behind the wall.

Utility

Water And Sewer: Who To Call For What

South Brunswick has its own water and sewer utility, which the township acquired in 1971, and its franchise areas include parts of North Brunswick. The water comes from wells in the Farrington Aquifer plus purchased water from New Jersey American Water.

If water stops across your street, call the township first. If the problem is only in your house, a closed main valve, a failed service line or a leak inside may be the cause. After hours, the township sends utility emergencies through its police dispatch, and we take it from there on the private side. Our leak detection page explains the meter test that separates the two.

Before we arrive

What To Do While You Wait

These steps work across the township. Keep them on the fridge and share them with your family.

Stop the water

Close the main valve, then open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.

Protect your floors

Lift rugs and move electronics from wet rooms.

Mind the electrics

Stay clear of outlets in wet areas and switch off power only if the panel is dry.

Note the details

Write down fixtures affected, noises and smells. Take photos.

Gas smell? Leave first and call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 from outside.

Low ground

Nature, Mill Ponds And Low Ground

South Brunswick has Davidson's Mill Pond, the Kingston Mill Historic District and the 1,078 acres of Pigeon Swamp State Park. Homes near ponds, streams and wetlands sit on low ground, and basements there need a good drainage plan.

A sump pump with a battery backup is the standard answer, and waterproofing work handles the rest. Older farmhouses and former farmland lots may also have private wells or septic systems, so tell us when you call if your property is not on the public system.

Business

Routes 1, 130, 27 And 32: Business Plumbing

Routes 1, 130, 27 and 32 carry the commercial strips, warehouses and offices of South Brunswick. After-hours restroom floods and restaurant backups are the calls we see. Our commercial plumbing team works with owners and property managers, and landlords can approve work by phone.
