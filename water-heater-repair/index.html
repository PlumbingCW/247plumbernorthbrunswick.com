If you have no hot water or a heater is leaking, call for emergency water heater repair in North Brunswick, NJ. The 24/7 line is answered at night, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

While you wait, turn off the cold water supply valve above the tank if it is leaking. Switch an electric unit off at the breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot or off. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 before you call us.

Many failures are repairable: a thermostat, a heating element, a gas valve or a thermocouple. Others point to a tank at the end of its life, and then water heater replacement is the honest answer. We tell you which one applies.

Urgent signs

Urgent Signs Your Water Heater Cannot Wait

Call the 24/7 line now if

  • ✓Water is leaking from the tank body or the bottom, not just a fitting.
  • ✓The tank is hot to the touch at the relief valve and steam or hot water is discharging.
  • ✓You smell gas near the heater, or the pilot keeps going out and a burner flame looks yellow.
  • ✓The unit makes loud banging or popping and the water is rusty.
  • ✓There is no hot water at all and the household includes infants, elderly people or someone with a medical need.

A slow drip at a fitting is different from a tank that is leaking. A fitting can often be tightened or replaced. A rusted tank cannot be patched.

Heater types

Gas, Electric And Hybrid Heaters

A gas heater depends on a pilot or igniter, a thermocouple, a gas control valve and proper venting. A failed thermocouple is a small part, but a venting problem is a safety issue, and a heater with a blocked vent should stay off until it is fixed.

An electric heater has two heating elements and two thermostats. Often only one fails, which is why the water turns lukewarm instead of cold. A hybrid heat pump unit adds a compressor and fan, and we check the condensate drain, filter and airflow before we blame the tank.

If a part costs a small fraction of a new heater and the tank is sound, repair is sensible. If the tank is leaking, we say so and move to replacement or to a tankless option.

Tank life

Anode Rods And How Long A Tank Lasts

Inside a steel tank sits a sacrificial anode rod. It corrodes so the tank does not. Once the rod is used up, the steel starts to rust, and rusty hot water or a rotten-egg smell is the usual first sign.

Checking the rod is a short job on a tank that is not too old. Swapping a spent rod can add years to a sound tank. On a tank that has already started to weep at the seams, a new rod comes too late, and replacement is the better use of money.

Ask for the rod to be inspected at any repair visit. It costs little to look and tells you a lot about what the inside of the tank looks like.

Safety

Temperature Settings And Scald Safety

A tank set too low can let bacteria grow, and one set too high can scald a child in seconds. Many plumbers suggest a setting that balances safety and comfort, and an anti-scald mixing valve at the heater lets you store hotter water while delivering a safer temperature to the taps.

If the water runs hot and cold at random, the thermostat or a mixing valve may be failing. A thermostat is a small part, but it is the part that decides whether the water is safe, so it is worth fixing properly.

Repair or replace

Is It Worth Repairing?

A tank heater usually lasts roughly a decade in many homes. The age is on the data plate, and the serial number often encodes the month and year. A newer unit with a bad part is a repair. An older unit with rust at the base is a replacement.

Our rule is simple: if the repair is cheap and the tank is sound, repair. If the tank is leaking, or the unit is old and needs a costly part, put that money toward a new heater. We give you both numbers so the choice is yours.

Local water

Hard Water And Your Tank In North Brunswick

The township's water utility, operated by Veolia, reported hardness of 67.5 ppm as calcium carbonate in its 2022 water quality report. That is about 3.9 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard. Scale builds up slowly rather than quickly, but it does build up.

Flushing a tank heater once a year removes sediment before it insulates the burner or covers the bottom element. If it has never been flushed, call us before you try it yourself, since a long-neglected drain valve can break when opened.

The relief valve and its discharge pipe are code items under the New Jersey plumbing subcode, the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, and they are the first parts we check.

Pricing

What Affects The Cost Of Water Heater Repair

The failed part

A thermocouple or relief valve costs far less than a gas control valve.

Fuel type

Gas, electric and hybrid units use different parts and need different skills.

Age and model

Older units may need parts that take time to source.

Venting and code issues

A gas unit with a venting problem needs correction before it runs.

Hour of the visit

A night or holiday call costs more than a scheduled visit.

Insurance, honestly

Homeowners insurance typically does not cover a worn-out heater. It may cover the water damage if the tank burst suddenly. Take photos before cleanup and call your insurer.
