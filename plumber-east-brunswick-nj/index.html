Local housing

Houses Built In A Fast-Growing Township

East Brunswick had 5,699 residents in 1950. By 1960 it had 19,965, after the Turnpike extension opened in 1952 and families moved in along Route 18 and the Exit 9 corridor. The 2020 Census counted 49,715 people across 22.36 square miles.

That growth means many homes now carry supply lines and drain lines from the same few decades of building. Copper supply pipes develop pinhole leaks, cast iron drains narrow with scale, and original water heaters finish their lives. If your house is one of them, repiping and a sewer camera inspection are worth a conversation before a failure forces the issue.

Neighborhoods We Serve In East Brunswick

The township includes Dunhams Corner, Herberts Corner, Lawrence Brook Manor, Fairview Knolls, Farrington Lake Heights, Orchard Heights and Washington Heights, among other areas. Heavenly Farms and the Ireland Brook Conservation Area give parts of town a wooded, low-lying character.

Homes near Lawrence Brook, Farrington Lake and Westons Mill Pond sit near moving water, and basements near the lower ground are the ones that need a working sump. Prepare

Before We Arrive

A few minutes of preparation protect your home while the plumber is on the way. These steps apply to most East Brunswick emergencies.

Shut off the water

Close the main valve near the meter or where the line enters the house.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the wet spot.

Stay dry and safe

Stay out of standing water near outlets. Leave if you smell gas.

Take photos

Photograph the damage and the source before cleanup for your insurer.

Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are involved and whether the basement has a floor drain or sump. That one detail tells us which tools to bring.
