New fixtures, new lines and remodel plumbing all need the right pipe, the right slope and the right permit. We install it and test it before we leave.

Written by the North Brunswick 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated October 13, 2026

Plumbing installation in North Brunswick, NJ covers the planned work that adds or replaces fixtures and lines. Think new sinks, toilets, showers, laundry hookups, outdoor spigots and basement bathrooms.

A good install begins with a plan. We confirm where the supply and drain will run, whether the drain has the slope and capacity for the new fixture, and what the township may need to inspect.

If the job exposes old supply lines, we discuss repiping. If the project involves heating water, see water heater installation. For anything failing right now, use the 24/7 line.

Planning

Planning A Basement Or Remodel Bathroom

A basement bathroom is the most common surprise in older homes. If the fixtures sit below the main drain, waste has to be lifted by a sewage ejector pump, which requires a sealed pit and a vent. The pit needs to be sized for the fixtures and the pump needs electrical power.

Ceiling height, slab thickness and the location of the main line decide how hard the job is. We walk the space first and draw up a route before we quote.

Adding a fixture or a bathroom? Call and we will plan it with you.

Rules

Code And Permits

New Jersey's plumbing subcode adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition. That governs trap size, venting, pipe sizing and backflow protection on new work.

New installs generally need permits and inspections. Ask the North Brunswick building department what your project requires. A licensed plumber is required for plumbing work under New Jersey law, and licensees must display their license number on invoices and advertising.

Fixtures

Choosing Fixtures That Fit

Buy fixtures with the rough-in size in mind. A toilet with a 10-inch or 14-inch rough-in will not fit a flange at 12 inches. A wall-mounted faucet needs different valves from a deck-mounted one.

If you are unsure, bring us the spec sheet or call before you buy. We can often point out a fit problem before it becomes a return. For repairs to existing fixtures, see toilet repair and faucet repair.
