Water heater installation in North Brunswick, NJ is easiest when it is scheduled. You choose the time, compare options and avoid the rush of a cold-water morning.

This page covers planned installs, including new construction, a basement remodel, a fuel change and an upgrade. If your tank is leaking now, see emergency water heater replacement, and if you only have a failed part, see water heater repair.

We walk through size, fuel, location and venting, and we put the price in front of you before we start.

Planning

Choosing The Right Heater For The House

Three questions decide most installs: how many people use hot water at the same hour, what fuel the house has and where the heater will sit. The answers narrow the field quickly.

Scope

What A Proper Install Includes

On every install we check

  • ✓A temperature and pressure relief valve with a proper discharge pipe.
  • ✓An expansion tank where the system is closed.
  • ✓Correct venting, combustion air and clearances for a gas unit.
  • ✓Shut-off valves and new supply connectors.
  • ✓A drain pan and drain line where leaks could cause damage.
  • ✓A carbon monoxide alarm recommendation near gas appliances.

Permit rules vary. Ask the North Brunswick building department what a new install or fuel change needs. We can tell you what documents the inspector may want.

Upgrades

Fuel Changes And Upgrades

Moving from electric to gas, or from a tank to a tankless unit, changes the work behind the wall. A gas line may need a larger diameter, a vent may need to go through the roof or wall, and an electric heater may need a new circuit.

That work is possible in many houses. It is also the reason we look at the space before we quote. For a tankless heater, see our tankless water heater page. If you also plan to replace old supply pipes, ask about repiping at the same time.

Placement

Where The Heater Lives: Basements, Closets And Garages

Location shapes the install. A basement heater has room and a floor drain nearby. A closet unit needs combustion air, clearances and a drain pan. A garage unit may need protection from impact and, in cold weather, from freezing.

If you want to move the heater, tell us early. A new location can mean new gas, water, vent and drain runs. It is often worth it for a remodel, and we price it before we start so there are no surprises.

Local water

A Note On North Brunswick Water

The township utility, operated by Veolia, reported hardness of 67.5 ppm as calcium carbonate in 2022, about 3.9 grains per gallon. That is moderately hard and a good reason to plan a yearly flush from the first day.

Pricing

What Affects An Installation Price

Heater type

Standard tank, tankless and hybrid units differ widely in cost.

Venting

A new or longer vent run adds parts and labor.

Gas and electrical work

A larger gas line or a new circuit is extra work.

Code items

Expansion tank, drain pan and discharge piping may be required.

Access and location

A tight closet or a long run from the old location adds time.

Insurance, honestly

A planned install is not covered by homeowners insurance. Some utilities and state programs offer rebates on high-efficiency units, so check before you buy.
