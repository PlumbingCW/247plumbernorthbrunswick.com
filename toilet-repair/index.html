Emergency Toilet Repair in North Brunswick, NJ

A toilet that overflows, leaks at the base or will not stop running is wasting water and risking your floor. We fix the cause, not just the symptom.

An overflowing toilet is a classic case for emergency toilet repair in North Brunswick, NJ. The 24/7 line is open, and we offer same-day appointments when available.

Turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop refilling. If it is overflowing, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down. Do not flush again, and put towels around the base. If the water is not just from the bowl, it could be a main line issue, and you should see drain cleaning.

Most toilet problems come down to a few parts, and a licensed plumber can usually fix them in one visit.

Base leaks

Leaks At The Base

Water at the base is not always a seal failure. It can come from the supply connection, the tank bolts or condensation. A true base leak means the wax ring or the flange is bad, and the floor under the toilet may be damaged.

Do not keep using a toilet that rocks. It breaks the seal, lets sewer gas into the room and can rot the subfloor over time. A reset with a new seal is a quick job that costs far less than repairing a floor.

High bills

Why A Running Toilet Raises Your Bill

A running toilet can waste a surprising amount of water with no sound beyond a hiss. Add a few drops of food coloring to the tank. If the color reaches the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking.

If your bill climbed and the toilets check out, our leak detection service looks for other leaks in the house.

Decision

Repair Or Replace The Toilet?

A toilet with a cracked bowl or tank cannot be repaired. An old model that needs several parts may cost more to fix than to replace. A modern low-flow toilet with a good bowl design clears better and uses less water.

We carry common parts, so most repairs finish in one trip. If you prefer a new toilet, tell us the rough-in measurement and we will advise.

Pricing

What Affects The Cost Of Toilet Repair

The part

A flapper costs little. A new toilet or flange repair costs more.

Clog or damage

A simple auger job is less than a subfloor repair.

Flange condition

A broken flange needs a repair kit or replacement.

Access

A tight bathroom or wall-hung tank takes longer.

Hour of the visit

A night or holiday call costs more than a daytime appointment.

Insurance, honestly

A worn toilet is a maintenance item and is not covered. Water damage from a sudden overflow may be covered. Document the damage with photos.
